TIME has named 5B one of the world’s top greentech companies of 2025 – coming in at #36! 🏆 TIME , in partnership with Statista, analysed more than 8000 greentech companies globally, considering and scoring them on their positive impact, financial strength, and innovation. The top 250 companies come from more than 30 countries around the world, and 5B is one of the top scoring Australian companies to have made it – an absolute honour.
