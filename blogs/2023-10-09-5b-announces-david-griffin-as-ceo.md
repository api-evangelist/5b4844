5B today announced that it has appointed David Griffin as chief executive officer, effective 11 October. “There is no better person than David to lead 5B through this time of transformation,” said 5B co-founder Chris McGrath. “He is a veteran of the renewable energy industry in Australia and around the world, with in-depth working knowledge of the complexity of the clean energy transition.
